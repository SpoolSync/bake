# Bake

Part of Pixora. Baker's math for home and professional kitchens, free and private.

- Formulas in grams with baker's percentages and hydration (starters are split into their flour and water).
- Scale by multiplier, number of pieces × weight, total dough, flour weight, or the one ingredient you're short of.
- Resize for a different pan (round, square, rectangle, bundt; inches or cm; uses volume if both depths are given).
- Cups to grams by ingredient, eggs rounded to whole eggs, water temperature for a target dough temperature.
- Ingredient prices per kg to cost a batch and each piece. A bake log with ratings and notes.
- Works offline. Encrypted on the phone with the Pixora passcode before it syncs.

Live: https://spoolsync.github.io/bake/
